    Multiplying and dividing negatives

    Which way will it go?

    In $3\times2$, the $3$ is a machine that stretches the arrow for $2$ to three times as long. A minus sign makes the machine flip the arrow to the other side of $0$.

    Shortcut card

    Even (0, 2, …) → positive.   Odd (1, 3, …) → negative.

    Mission A5

    Power shortcuts

    Too long to write!

    $2\times2\times2\times2\times2\times2\times2$ … just write $2^7$: “seven 2s”. The small number counts the bricks.

    Now let's find some shortcuts by counting.

    Count and discover
    Shortcut card (same big number only!)

    Multiply → add the small numbers: $\;3^2\times3^4=3^6$

    Divide → subtract: $\;5^6\div5^2=5^4$

    Power of a power → multiply: $\;(7^3)^2=7^6$
