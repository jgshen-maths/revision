Mission D1

Fractions and percentages of an amount

A bag of 20 sweets.

Would you rather have $\dfrac34$ of the bag, or $70\%$ of the bag? By the end of this mission you'll know, and you'll know why.

PART A

A fraction of an amount

Let's do it together
Play: the “of” machine
Shortcut card

“Of” means ×. $\dfrac34$ of $20=\dfrac34\times20$: divide by the bottom ($20\div4=5$), multiply by the top ($5\times3=15$).

PART B

A percentage of an amount

Let's do it together
Play: the 10% blocks
Shortcut card

$30\%=\dfrac{30}{100}=0.3$, and “of” means ×: $30\%$ of $120=120\times0.3=36$.

No calculator? $10\%$ is one block ($\div10$). $5\%$ is half a block.

PART C

One amount as a percentage of another

Let's do it together
Play: the score converter
Shortcut card

Part as a percentage of whole: $\dfrac{\text{part}}{\text{whole}}$, then make it out of 100, or do part $\div$ whole $\times100$.

🔒 Secret level: run it backwards (tap to unlock)

So far we went forwards: number → percentage of it. Can you go backwards? If $30\%$ of a number is $36$, what is the number?

Three ways to crack it
Shortcut card

Way 1 · blocks: $30\%=36$ → $10\%=12$ → $100\%=120$.

Way 2 · undo: forwards was $\times0.3$, so backwards is $\div0.3$: $36\div0.3=120$.

Way 3 · check forwards: $30\%$ of $120=36$ ✓. After an increase, the new amount is more than $100\%$ (e.g. $120\%$)!
