Mission B2

Rounding to significant figures

“About 24 000 fans watched the match.”

The real number was $23\,850$. Nobody wants to read every digit in a headline, so the reporter kept just the 2 most important digits and rounded. Those digits are called significant figures.

Mission B5

Dividing by a decimal

How many glasses?

A bottle holds $3$ litres. Each glass holds $0.5$ litres. How many glasses can you fill? That is $3\div0.5$.

Step by step

Mental maths tricks

$48\times0.99$ without a calculator?

It sounds hard. With the right trick it takes five seconds. Here are three tricks.

Learn the tricks
Shortcut card
